I think the purpose of that ceremony was to ensure that I would be deemed acceptable to the patriarchal society and made into someone who was no longer single and solitary, but instead, enmeshed with another person and obliged to be accountable to that person (as a way of keeping me under the community's control more than I would be if I were to remain single). I remember thinking that going along with it would not be too bad provided that I did not give into pressure to have children nor to renounce my personal interests and hobbies (which seemed possible because apparently the guy I was being pressured to marry was supposedly not super controlling nor even all that enthusiastic about partnering up either (other than to avoid social and financial penalties which seem to implicitly be enforced upon singles as a way of pressuring them to marry and birth and/or raise their replacements)). What caused me to rebel against the whole thing was thinking about how I did not want my identity to include being someone's wife. The word "wife" repulsed me enough to easily turn down all the "benefits" of going along with the community's plans and to risk being ostracized and punished.

To be clear, I am very staunchly devoted to remaining relationally single and erring on the side of also remaining celibate. That way I can be sure to avoid sexually transmitted diseases (including urinary tract infections especially (which I've suffered chronic bouts of in the past as a consequence of "routine relational maintenance" with a penis-baring individual who neglected to be adequately hygienic and likely had sexual relations without my knowledge with other people who likely were not adequately hygienic or disease-free)). I would say that what I am is post-sexual; formerly interested in "normal" human sexual relations (to a moderate extent) but the costs of such always seemed to outweigh the benefits to me personally. I also say that I am post-sexual because I see sexual reproduction as an arcane and eventually obsolete means for keeping the human population from vanishing (due to advancements in stem cell cloning of body parts to keep individual humans alive and able-bodied for hundreds if not thousands of years and also by outsourcing sexual reproduction to lab-grown gametes and artificial wombs in a manner which optimizes population size, genetic quality, and resource distribution rather than leaving such things up to chance and rat-like capriciousness (or ape-like rape-adjacent coercion)).

Also, in a world where population size calibration is more deliberate and non-coercive towards existing and future human individuals, poverty and crime is likely to plummet due to there being virtually zero resource shortages, lopsided resource distribution, institutional marginalization, and human trafficking for reproductive purposes (which was the norm for the past few thousand years).
