A less criminality and less antisociality sketch of my chronic (more than eight consecutive years) head entrapment (and, to a much lesser extent, body entrapment) is the rather impersonal and naturalistic scenario of finding myself wedged in place after being corralled by various forms of pressure into remaining confined to as tiny of a spatial volume as possible after having lived a much more expansively free existence due to ruthless and impersonal competition algorithms in nature which cause less socially and less physically powerful beings to retreat into hiding where they essentially rot to death quietly and invisibly according to the majority of people deemed to be sufficiently able-bodied and socially approved of to inhabit the public spaces of society without (yet) being subject to intense coercive pressure to "disappear" (without obviously committing any murder) so as to "protect the impressionable youth" from examples of maladaption. All I would have to "enjoy" in such circumstances is the eroticization of my humiliation, entrapment, and loss of power.
