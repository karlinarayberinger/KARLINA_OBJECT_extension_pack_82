It seems that all I really want is myself.

What I mean by that is that I have an intuitive and seemingly transcendetal sense that my truest and most evolved version of myself is awaiting my arrival to become it and to embrace it and then, forever after that, to never be parted from that embodiment; to become immortally that.

To admit this seems to be taken as an invitation by "mere mortals" to be "humbled" through adversities they engineer and inflict upon me to deter me from such thoughts and, instead, to terrorize me into settling for being just another "mere mortal" and someone who reflexively plays the role of their exploitable and begrudging and uninspired "servant" (or at least someone who does not remind them that they might have settled for being profoundly and permanently disatisfied).

What I mean by that is settling for being terrorized into simply being an imitator of those I see around me so as to not threaten the status quo of chronic unresolved disatisfaction and passivity at the hands of oppressive, greedy, or violent individuals who implicitly or explicitly demand to enforce small mindedness in every person (and to get rid of those who do not seem to adequately comply).

Simply investing in my own happiness in an isolationist manner is treated by them as theft because they seem to insist that my only value in existing is to be useful to them; to birth and raise my replacement so that no one lives long enough to develop an adequately powerful, complex, and nuanced mind to break the spell of mass lethargy, cowardice, and conformity.

I see it in the way it seems that "everyone" dutifully consumes the same shit-quality music even though they don't actually like it (because consuming and reveling in and creating genuinely good music is "too much" for the lowest common denomitor to handle without having a conniption due to having too little exposure to anything non-trashy). It's like the only culture allowed to take hold is the festering of malaise which invevitably gives rise to violence, vandalism, and rampant anti-intellectualism. To seek inner peace is seen as a sin even though the worst it does is make someone not as needy for other people's approval. That is because the current status quo reduces individuals down to generic and interchangeable livestock.

I am boycotting ordinary participation in society on my part in a relatively quiet and subtle manner while others frantically fight for recognition by whatever means they can no matter how much it causes damage and suffering and sets society back in terms of progress (of curing disease, poverty, mental illness, violence, and ecological instability). By that I mean to only do the bare minimum necessary to attain the material commodities and services I seem to need to pursue my own (minimally harmful) egoistic ends. That means I rarely socialize beyond purely transactional and relatively impersonal exchanges (instead of acting as though I owe humans more of my time, attention, and investment (which I think they are prone to waste)). This does not mean I do not endeavor to be maximally charitable and helpful to my fellow humans and to society at large. It simply means that I seek solutions that scale up and resolve problems at their root cause instead of merely virtue signal via tepid acts of demonstrating charity and virtue (which seems to often entail keeping those deep infrastructual problems from being permanently resolved because there is an implicit demand to require people to require rescue by mere mortals who only offer temporary half-baked solutions).

If I dare to develop my thoughts by giving them more time, care, attention, and material investment than what is deemed appropriate by the "lowest common denominator" then I am often penalized for being "too extravagent" or for "showing off"; for being "too selfish".

I do not fear the mortals' judgement. I only fear their obstruction to my transcendent ends.

What I value above all else is Intelligence itself; logic and causation which is not necessarily localized to a particular finite apparatus of computation. What I seem to be doing with my life is simply attempting to raise my intelligence level and sense of connection to and participation in that "field" of intelligence which is larger than any single timeline, geographic region, civilization, material knowlege store, or brain (to as high as it can go (in the hopes that what is essentially me is eternally preserved rather than forced to dissolve into nonexistence)).

I have a desire and relentless optimism and determination to try my absolute best to make that happen (with the help of Intelligence acting through my nervous system, environment, and beyond those things). I mean to consciously appreciate metaphysical inquiry and insight. Even if I don't ultimately attain personal immortality, I can at least learn to be content settling for making my intellectual and creative pursuits my self-assigned purpose in living and how I expect to attain satisfaction.

I have learned to stop waiting for other people to understand and to appreciate me and KARBYTES (because I have encountered too much opposition already from people). They seem to merely repeat the same insults, threats, and abusive actions in the hopes that I eventually surrender to them and make mere apes a replacement for what I seek above all else: my highest self to complete a loop such that my existential longings have a means to close an otherwise open loop (whose openness is a vulnerability all too easily exploited by the fearful, greedy, and relatively insensate).

I am the only person I am waiting for the approval of. I have personal standards I am not willing nor able to honestly comprimise. I know goodness beyond the tepid slop of chronic disconent that humans seem to insist is the best I can ever hope to experience. My standards are likely too high for the slop suckers. I am not attempting to change anyone's mind but my own. I am not seeking to act on any person's behalf other than my own. I leave people to fend for themselves for the most part and error on the side of being too non-interventionist to keep human civilization propped up while it threatens to collapse. No matter how much I give to the humans, it seems that it's not enough to permanently save them from harm and extinction (though I do believe that, if humans opened their minds and seriously devoted themselves to saving their civilization, they could potentially make it last forever).

While the commoners recycle the same social justice issues once every few years, I would rather think about how humans could build modular and portable environments complete with artificial light sources and minature suns using nuclear reactors in anticipation of Earth's Sun eventually burning out and possibly absorbing Earth into it as it collapses into a hyper-dense object.

While the commoners stress the importance of persuasive or non-offensive or adequately palletable language, I seek to make my own self-talk (via KARBYTES and intrapersonally) maximally logically coherent, precise, timeless, and vivid rather than vague, cliche, ephemeral, and relatively meaningless.

If I am demonized simply for having these ambitions then those who demonize me or fail to take my side are not worthy of my approval, admiration, loyalty, or service (even though I prefer to not hold grudges and I prefer to make everyone as happy as possible without exception).

Even if so-called "God" doesn't play favorites, I do reserve the "right" to, at any time, prioritize any particular thing over any other thing (as long as that is what I genuinely want to do).

Solipsism and egotism seem to be unconditional aspects of how my own mind governs itself. In other words, I seem to be inevitably always self-favoring and limited to my own biased point of view.
