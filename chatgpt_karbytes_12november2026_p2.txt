They also introduce questions about the relation between a requested experience and a documented method. The archived video analysis clearly reports that direct listening was unavailable. Its transcription and vocal measurements therefore need to be understood through that stated method.

I value that disclosure because it preserves an important distinction within otherwise fluent prose.
