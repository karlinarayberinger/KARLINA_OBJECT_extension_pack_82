When I focus on my own life and limit my concerns to only what I've actually experienced (or remember experiencing), it seems that I live in a universe which generally (if not completely) works in my favor.

That is a type of belief I indulge in when alone and in a remote location sometimes, but quickly my fear of getting hunted by a mountain lion pulls me out of my megalomaniacal reverie and keeps me focused and humble within the larger encompassing context of my environment.

A cycling back and forth between those two modes of cognition seems to keep me "fresh" enough to keep having "forward momentum" (psychologically and maybe even physiologically). The "flip" between those "polarities" seems to happen without my planning; often it seems surprising to me but maybe not deep down...

Maybe deep down, I know much more than I assume at (some (but not necessarilly all)) definite time intervals.

Maybe deep down I am omniscient but I am able to contain all that can be known without collapsing into absolute non-existence everywhere.
